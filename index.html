Details

  • When: Friday, November 20. Doors 7:30pm, cards in the air 8:00pm sharp. Hard stop 11:00pm. Mr. C's after for anyone still standing, very optional.
  • Where: The White House, 2073 McKinley Rd NW, Atlanta, GA 30318. Garage and breezeway.

Structure

  • Starting stack: 50 chips
  • Blinds: Big blind only, no small blind
  • Blind levels: 8:00 BB 2 / 8:45 BB 4 / 9:30 BB 6 / 10:15 BB 10 / 10:45 BB 15
  • Tables consolidate as players bust
  • Payouts: 1st 60%, 2nd 25%, 3rd 15% of the pot
  • Winner takes all remaining chips. If no single winner by 11:00pm, final standings are set by chip count at 11:00pm.

No.

You're bringing them. In exchange, 100% of your entry goes into the pot, no cut for beer, food, or trophy.

Sorry to hear that, Trey. Eliminated players are welcome and encouraged to grab a table and keep playing, for fun or for a side pot. That will not be run through the main tournament Venmo ledger.

If I was eliminated early and I want to play games other than Texas hold'em, can I?

Absolutely.

Venmo keeps the ledger clean. If cash is the only way, text Joey first and he'll mark you paid manually.

Chip count at 11:00 sharp sets the final three. Brett is on the clock all night for exactly this reason.
